What this is

A free QR code generator that runs entirely in your browser. There's no signup, no email collection, no watermark on your downloads, and no ads anywhere on the page. Encode URLs, Instagram profiles, plain text, phone numbers, SMS, and Wi-Fi credentials.

The QR codes generated here are static — they encode your data directly into the pattern, so they never expire and they keep working as long as the underlying destination does.

FAQ

Common questions

7 Questions Answered
Are the QR codes really free? add
Yes. Every feature is free — no signup, no premium tier, and no watermark on the QR codes you download. The SVG, PNG, and JPEG files are yours to use however you'd like, including commercially.
Do these QR codes ever expire? add
No. The QR codes generated here are static — they encode your data directly into the pattern. They keep working forever, as long as the destination (URL, network, etc.) is still valid.
Can I use these QR codes commercially? add
Absolutely. There are no usage restrictions. Print them on packaging, business cards, posters, restaurant menus, signage, products — anywhere you like.
Does this track me or my QR data? add
The QR generation runs entirely locally in your browser. Your input never reaches any server.
Should I download SVG, PNG, or JPEG? add
SVG is a vector format that stays sharp at any size — best for print, signage, business cards, and design tools. PNG is a lossless raster format with transparency support — best for web displays. JPEG is a universally compatible compressed format with a solid background — best for email, social sharing, and photo documents.
How do I make a Wi-Fi QR code? add
Pick the Wi-Fi tab, enter your network name (SSID) and password, and choose the encryption type. WPA/WPA2 is the most common. The QR code can then be scanned by most modern phones to connect to the network automatically.
Will the QR still work if I change colors? add
Yes, as long as there's enough contrast between the foreground (the dots) and the background. Dark dots on a light background scan most reliably. Always test scan it before printing — especially with custom colors.
